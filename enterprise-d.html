USS Enterprise-D Bridge

3D Environment · Modeling · Texturing · Lighting

A personal 3D recreation of the USS Enterprise-D bridge from Star Trek: The Next Generation. The project began near the end of my studies and was later revisited in Blender, where I refined the geometry, materials, textures and lighting using improved reference material.

The entire environment was modeled from scratch using a subdivision-surface workflow. Reconstructing the bridge's curved architecture and maintaining continuity across its many rounded transitions was the main modeling challenge. LCARS panel graphics and textures were also created specifically for the environment.

LCARS Panel Detail

The LCARS graphics were reconstructed in Krita from photographic references of original production props. Because the source images were often grainy and photographed at oblique angles, they were first corrected for perspective and scale before the interface artwork was rebuilt as clean layered graphics for the 3D environment.
