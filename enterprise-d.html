USS Enterprise-D Bridge

3D Environment · Modeling · Texturing · Lighting

>>>>>> 832b2e3 (Update portfolio) alt="USS Enterprise-D bridge 3D environment">

A personal 3D recreation of the USS Enterprise-D bridge from Star Trek: The Next Generation. The project began near the end of my studies and was later extensively revisited in Blender. The current version refines the geometry, UVs, materials, textures, lighting and display graphics using improved reference material.

The environment was modeled from scratch using a subdivision-surface workflow. Reconstructing the bridge's curved architecture and maintaining continuity across its rounded transitions was the main modeling challenge. The project also includes custom LCARS graphics, emissive display materials, detailed command-area assets and smaller environmental details created specifically for the scene.

Custom LCARS Graphics

>>>>>> 832b2e3 (Update portfolio) alt="Custom LCARS graphics integrated into the USS Enterprise-D bridge" loading="lazy">

All LCARS graphics shown in the project were recreated from scratch in Krita based on photographic and screen references. Where source material was soft or partially unreadable, I reconstructed layouts, labels and interface elements to preserve the restrained 1990s TNG visual language. No production artwork from the series was used directly in the final textures.
