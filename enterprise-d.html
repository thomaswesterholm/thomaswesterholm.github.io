USS Enterprise-D Bridge

3D Environment · Modeling · Texturing · Lighting

A personal 3D recreation of the USS Enterprise-D bridge from Star Trek: The Next Generation. The project began during my studies and was later revisited and extensively refined in Blender, with updated geometry, materials, textures and lighting.

The entire environment was modeled from scratch using a subdivision workflow, based on blueprints, screen references and archival material. Particular attention was given to the bridge's complex curved architecture and LCARS panels, with the latter recreated in Krita from photographic references of the original props.
