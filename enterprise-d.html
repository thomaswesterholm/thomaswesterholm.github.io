USS Enterprise-D Bridge

3D Environment · Modeling · Texturing

A 3D recreation of the bridge of the USS Enterprise-D, created as an environment modeling project.

The project focused on recreating the overall architecture, interior forms, furniture and visual language of the original bridge while building the environment as a complete 3D scene.
