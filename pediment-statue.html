Pediment Sculpture

3D Sculpture · Modeling · Materials · Lighting

A classical-inspired sculptural study focused on anatomy, form, architectural framing and presentation. The figure was developed as a heroic stone sculpture and integrated into a custom pediment structure with columns, arches and a monumental base.

The project was modeled and sculpted in Blender, with separate material treatments for the statue and architectural elements. Lighting and post-production were developed to emphasize the figure's large forms while retaining the monument as a readable part of the composition.
