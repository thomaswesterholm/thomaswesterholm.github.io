Resort Building

3D Environment · Modeling · UV Mapping · Texturing · Lighting

This project began as an environment art test based on three supplied reference views of a building. The original version was modeled in Maya as a relatively simple architectural shell, with the brief focusing on modeling, texturing, efficient geometry and UV use.

I later revisited the project in Blender and expanded it into a more complete environment. The building was refined with additional geometry, modeled windows and entrance details, interior volume behind the facade, materials, textures and lighting. I also created the surrounding scene and supporting props, including outdoor furniture, sun loungers and parasols.

Original Art Test Reference

The supplied reference images established the building's overall architecture and proportions while leaving room to interpret and develop details beyond the original concept.

Clay Render

Wireframe

UV Mapping

The building was fully UV mapped with attention to consistent scale and efficient use of texture space. A checker texture was used to evaluate distortion and texel density across the architectural surfaces.

Environment Props

Supporting assets were modeled for the expanded Blender scene to give the building scale and context while keeping the architecture as the primary focus.
